3. Hosts and visitors

If you host, you decide who comes in and can remove or block them at any time. You also choose what each visitor can do: Building & decorating (torches and decorations), Farm tools, Animal care (including riding your horse), Storage, Dropped items, Combat, Ogre War, and Missions. Permissions you give a player are remembered for all future visits until you remove them, in the Wi-Fi menu or its Remembered list. Please only give permissions you are comfortable with. Things visitors do under them can change your world.

Visitors' torches and decorations stay in the host's world. They are saved there permanently, up to 8 decorations per visitor and 24 per world, and decorations count toward the host's Home Rating, even if the visitor is later blocked. A host can remove them, and a visitor with Building & decorating can remove their own. A removed decoration goes back to the visitor; a yard piece comes back as a 50% coin refund, the same as in solo play. A visitor's removed torch is not refunded.

If you visit, you bring your character and keep what you earn. The host's world and rules are theirs. Items you leave in a world you can no longer reach, such as a decoration on hold, may be lost.

Everyone who visits can see pictures hung in the host's world, including the host's own photos. Hosts are responsible for what they hang.

5. Report, mute and block

  • Report: tap Report on a player in the Wi-Fi menu, or Report chat in the chat box. Reporting also mutes and blocks that player for you. A report does not carry chat text or pictures, so if you can add details or a screenshot, email them to staycalmandgrind@gmail.com with the player's name or ID. You can report by email too, including illegal content, if you do not play.
  • Mute: silences that player's voice and hides their chat on your device until the game closes.
  • Block: lasts until you undo it at the Garden Line Station. They cannot visit you or ask to. If you are a guest, you cannot stop the host letting someone in; their voice and chat are hidden for you, and you can leave.
  • Hosts can also remove a player at once with Kick.

We aim to review reports within 24 hours, sooner for serious ones. If content or behaviour breaks these terms, we will act, which can mean a warning, or restricting or removing the account of the player responsible. Where the law requires it, we also pass reports to the authorities.

A word filter covers chat and names, but filters miss things. Live voice and pictures cannot be checked before they reach others, so report what you find. If someone is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number.
