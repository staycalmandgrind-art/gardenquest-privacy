What you'll do

  • Grow your farmPlant and water dozens of crops, raise animals, plant an orchard, and build the farm up season by season.
  • Know everyoneA town full of neighbours with their own homes, routines and stories. Make friends, give gifts, get invited in.
  • Explore the forestPast the farm gate there's a deep forest to delve, with gear to craft, creatures to meet and plenty worth bringing home.
  • Play togetherVisit a friend's garden, help with the watering, and take on the rec centre's sports and mini-games.
