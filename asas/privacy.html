ASAS Privacy Policy

Effective November 27, 2026

This policy describes how SW12H Studios handles information in Adonis Scribe and Scan (ASAS).

Documents, scans, and exports

You can use ASAS without creating an account. ASAS stores your drafts, imported documents, original scans, and recognized text within the app on your device.

ASAS uses your camera when you choose to scan a document. Google ML Kit recognizes text on your device. Google may receive information about SDK usage and performance, and the SDK may connect to Google to obtain component updates. Google ML Kit privacy information

ASAS prepares exports on your device. You choose where to save or share each exported copy. Once you send a copy to another app or service, its handling depends on that destination. Your operating system and backup settings can affect whether your app data is backed up.

Voice features and references

ASAS saves brainstorming notes, reference images, and named document versions on your device.

When you start dictation, ASAS asks for microphone access and uses your device’s speech recognition service. ASAS requests on-device recognition when available. If the device uses an online speech service, that provider may process your audio under its own privacy settings and terms. ASAS adds the transcript to your document when you choose to insert it. Read aloud uses your device’s installed text-to-speech service.

Advertising and privacy choices

ASAS uses Google AdMob to display ads. The free plan can show full-screen ads during document switches. Google’s ad software may collect and share your IP address and an approximate location derived from it, advertising or other device identifiers, ad and app interactions, and diagnostics. Google uses this information to deliver and measure advertising, analyze performance, and prevent fraud. How Google uses data from apps

In About ASAS, choose Ad privacy options when a consent form is available for your region. On iPhone or iPad, you can manage app tracking permission in your device’s privacy settings. Android provides controls for your advertising ID in device settings.

Retention and deletion

Your library stays on your device until you delete your documents or remove the app. Deleting a document in ASAS also removes its brainstorming notes, reference images, and named versions. ASAS removes its original scan pages once no other document in your library uses them. Your device manages temporary export files and backups. Remove saved or shared copies from the places where you put them.

If you contact us, we use your email address and the information you send to respond and troubleshoot. We retain support correspondence as needed to handle and follow up on requests or meet legal obligations. Google handles retention of data it receives under its own policies. Google Privacy Policy
