ASAS Support

Get help with Adonis Scribe and Scan on iPhone, iPad, or Android.

Contact support

staycalmandgrind@gmail.com

Include your device model, operating system version, and what you were doing when the issue occurred. If you attach a screenshot, remove private information first.

Camera and text recognition

Use a device with a camera and allow camera access when your device asks. On Android, the scanner requires a supported device with Google Play services. To check the original image, open Original scan in the scanned document.

Saving and moving your documents

ASAS saves drafts and scans in the app’s library on your device. Use Export to save a copy to Files or another location before switching devices or deleting the app.

Ads and privacy

To report an ad, use its AdChoices control when available. You can also email support with the advertiser’s name and the approximate time the ad appeared.

Open About ASAS in the app to find Ad privacy options for your region. ASAS privacy policy
