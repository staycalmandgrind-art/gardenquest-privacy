Garden Quest RPG: Delete Your Account and Data

SW12H Studios · Effective date: November 5, 2026 (version 1.8.7)

Garden Quest RPG creates an online account only if you tap "Connect to friends". It is anonymous, with no real name, email, or password. Here is how to remove it, and how to remove what is on your device.

Delete your online account in the game

  1. Open Settings, find Online & your data, and choose Delete my online data. It is also at the Garden Line Station, under Your online data.
  2. Read the notice and tap Continue.
  3. Read the last check and tap Delete for good.

You need an internet connection. If your world is open or you are on a visit, close it or end it at the Garden Line Station first. Otherwise nothing is deleted, and the game tells you why. If the online service does not answer, the game says "Your account was NOT deleted" and offers Try again.

This cannot be undone. You lose your friend ID and your friends list, and this device is signed out. If you connect again later, you start with a new account and a new friend ID, and the game asks you to accept the Terms of Service again.

Request deletion without the game

If you have removed the game, cannot use it, or your account has been disabled, email staycalmandgrind@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my online data" and include your friend ID. You do not need to reinstall anything. We aim to answer within 24 hours, and we will complete the request within 30 days.

Please keep your friend ID if you plan to remove the game. It is shown at the Garden Line Station. Because the account has no real name or email, it is the only way we can find it. If you have lost it, a friend you gave it to may still have it. Without it, we cannot identify the account. We may ask for details to check the request is yours.

If your account has been disabled

A disabled account cannot sign in, so the game cannot delete it. If you choose Delete my online data, the game says so and shows your friend ID. Email us with that ID as described above, and we will delete the account.

You can still remove what is on your device. After that message, and before you close the game, choose Delete all on-device game data. It shows your friend ID once more, so please write it down. If you close the game first, choose Delete my online data again.

What is deleted, and what stays

DataWhat happens
Online account and player ID (Unity)Deleted.
Friends, friend requests, and blocks held by UnityDeleted. Your block list on your device is cleared too.
Voice service IDs, device ID, and shortened IP (Unity Vivox)Expire on their own within up to 30 days, according to Unity.
Reports about or by youMay be kept with Unity's moderation service as long as needed to keep players safe and enforce our rules, or where the law requires it.
Your worlds, progress, Gems, and photos on your deviceStay on your device until you delete them (see below). Deleting online data does not touch them.
Your age answer, and Unity's technical installation ID, on your deviceStay on your device until you delete everything (see below). Choosing 12 and under under "Change my age answer" in Settings also removes a remembered age answer. Neither is sent to us.
Things on other players' devicesStay. Their saves can keep your old ID and name in a host's remembered list or visit records. Decorations and torches you placed stay in their worlds and may no longer come back to you. We cannot reach other players' devices to remove these, but your old ID no longer connects to an online account.
Unfinished visit items, such as a decoration on hold or a locker depositMay be lost. Please finish visits first.
PurchasesStay with Apple or Google, under their rules. We cannot delete store records. Unity's store software also sends Unity purchase events that can be tied to your player ID, and deleting your online account may not remove them.
Ad data held by Google, achievements in Game Center or Play GamesHeld by Google or Apple. Use their account and privacy settings.

Delete what is on your device

  • One world: in the game's world list, tap Delete next to the world, then confirm. This removes that world's save, its visit records, and its framed picture copies. Your Gems, purchase record, Photo Mode pictures, settings, and block list are kept, since they are not tied to one world.
  • Everything, in the game: on the title screen, with no farm open, open Settings and choose Delete all on-device game data. Confirm, then type DELETE. This removes every farm, framed picture and photo in the game, your Gem balance and records, your settings, your age answer, and your block list. Close the game and open it again afterward. The game refuses while this device still has an online account, because this would lose the only way to reach that account. Use Delete my online data first. If your account has been disabled, see above.
  • Everything, without the game: remove the app from your device. This removes all of the game's own files.
  • Gems and Remove Ads: Gems cannot be restored afterward. Remove Ads can be restored with "Restore purchases".
  • Backups: your device's Android or iCloud backup may still hold saves. Manage them in your device settings.
  • Your photo library: Photo Mode pictures already saved to your device's photos stay there until you delete them.

Removing the app does not delete your online account. Use one of the routes above, in the game before you remove it or by email afterward.
