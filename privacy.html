Garden Quest — Cozy Farm: Privacy Policy

SW12H Studios · Effective date: October 14, 2026 (version 1.8)

Garden Quest — Cozy Farm ("the game") is built to be played, not to collect data. This policy describes what the game does and does not do with information on your device.

Data we collect

The game itself collects nothing. There are no accounts and no analytics SDKs, and we run no servers that receive your information. The only data handling happens inside services run by Google and Apple: Google's advertising service (described under "Advertising" below, and disabled entirely by the Remove Ads purchase), purchases through the App Store or Google Play, and the optional achievement services (Game Center or Google Play Games).

Game saves

Your farm, progress, and settings are saved locally on your device only. They are never uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes your saves.

Internet access

The free version performs a simple connectivity check (asking the operating system whether a network is available) to gate play sessions, and uses the connection to load advertisements. Players who purchase Remove Ads can play fully offline with no ads and no ad-related data handling at all.

Advertising

The free version shows advertisements served by Google AdMob: occasional interstitial ads during play, banner ads on the title screen and pause menu, and optional "watch an ad" bonus moments you choose to view. To serve and measure ads, Google's SDK may collect and process your device's advertising identifier, IP address, and coarse device information. This processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy; on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings at any time. On iPhone and iPad the game asks your permission first (App Tracking Transparency): if you choose not to allow tracking, your device's advertising identifier is not shared with Google, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. We do not receive or store this data ourselves. Purchasing Remove Ads removes all advertising — no ads are shown and the advertising SDK is never used to serve you ads again.

In-app purchases

The game offers the one-time Remove Ads purchase and Gem bundles (an in-game currency). Every purchase is processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play; we never see or store your payment details. A record of what you bought is kept on your device so the game can deliver it and restore Remove Ads. Some items bought with Gems contain random rewards; their exact odds are shown in the game before you buy.

Achievements

Official achievements use Game Center on iPhone and iPad and Google Play Games on Android. Signing in is handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies; the game only uses that sign-in to unlock and show your achievements.

Photos

When you take a picture in Photo Mode, the game keeps a copy inside the game for your in-game camera roll and picture frames, and saves it to your device's photos. On iPhone and iPad, iOS asks your permission before the game can add pictures to your photo library. The game only adds pictures; it never reads your photo library, and your pictures are never uploaded. Sharing a picture uses your device's own share sheet and only happens when you choose to share.

Mail Terminal (sending parcels to friends)

The Courier Station's Mail Terminal lets you send items to a friend's farm without any server: the game writes a parcel or friend link (and a parcel code your friend can type into their Mail Terminal instead) into a text message that you send from your own messaging app. If you add a friend, the name and phone number you type are saved only on your device, so the game can address the message for you. Your own phone number is included in the friend links you send only if you choose to enter it in the Mail Terminal, so your friends can message you back; it travels from you to your friend in your own text message and never reaches us. The game never reads your contacts, never sends a text by itself, and never uploads any of this information. A link or code can carry the short display name you choose (letters and numbers only).

Redeem and parcel codes

Gift codes and parcel codes are checked inside the game on your device. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, including children. The Android version contains casino-style arcade mini-games played with in-game coins only (declared as simulated gambling in its content rating); no real money can be won. The iPhone and iPad version contains no casino games.

Permissions

On Android, the game requests only the permissions Android requires to run a game (network state for the connectivity check described above). On iPhone and iPad, the game asks for App Tracking Transparency (for ads, which you can decline) and for permission to add Photo Mode pictures to your photo library. It does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location, or files.

Changes to this policy

If a future update changes any of the above, this page will be updated before that version ships, with the effective date revised.
