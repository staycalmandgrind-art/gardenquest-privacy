Garden Quest RPG: Privacy Policy

SW12H Studios · Effective date: November 5, 2026 (version 1.8.7)

The short version

  • Playing alone: your farm and progress are saved on your device. There is no sign-up, and we run no servers that receive your game data.
  • Playing online is optional. When you tap "Connect to friends", the game creates an anonymous account with Unity, which runs the online services.
  • Other players receive what you share. In a visit they get your name, your character, your chat, your voice if you turn it on, and, if you are the host, the pictures in your world, including your own photos.
  • The free version shows Google ads. Google handles device data to do that.
  • You can delete your online data. See Delete your data.

Voice chat

Voice is off until you tap "Join voice" and allow the microphone. You always join muted and choose when to talk. Anyone in the same area of the world in your visit can hear you. That can include people you do not know, because only the host has to be your friend. The mic mutes when the game goes to the background and while an ad plays. Only your own tap ever reopens your mic: not the end of an ad, a reconnect or moving to another room.

The game does not record voice, and we have not turned on Unity's server-side recording. Unity's documents say that voice is passed along without being stored, and that sign-in signals are encrypted but voice audio is encoded rather than encrypted on its way. Please do not say anything private over voice. See also "Reports and moderation", because a report can attach information from a voice channel.

Text chat and names

Chat goes to everyone in your visit, through the host's device. A word filter blocks some offensive words in chat and names; it is not perfect. The game keeps recent chat in memory during the visit and does not write it to a file. We do not store chat. Other players can of course take screenshots.

Photos and pictures

You can put your own photo in a picture frame. The system picker gives the game only the one picture you choose, and the game keeps a small copy for that world. It does not read the rest of your library.

Everyone who visits your world can see the pictures you hang in frames, including your own photos, and paintings on show there. The game sends them to visitors' devices through Unity's Relay service. The game says so on the frame screen and before you open your world. Visitors' games show the pictures during the visit and do not save them, but anyone can take a screenshot. To stop sharing a picture, take it out of its frame before opening your world. Please do not hang pictures of other people without their permission, or anything our Terms of Service forbid.

Photo Mode pictures are kept in the game's own camera roll. On your request they are added to your device's photos (iPhone and iPad ask your permission first). Sharing outside the game uses your device's share sheet.

Reports and moderation

You can report another player in the Wi-Fi menu or from the chat box. That also mutes and blocks them for you. A report goes to Unity's moderation service and contains: your player ID, their player ID, a reason, and the time with your time zone. It does not contain chat text or pictures. If you want to add those, you can email them to us.

If you were in voice, Unity's software also attaches both players' voice IDs, the channels you shared and the others in those channels. Unity says evidence can include event logs, and recorded audio where that is enabled, from a recent period (its documents say 15 or 45 minutes). So, if someone reports you, your voice channel activity may be reviewed.

Unity's Safe Text tool is on for this game. It analyses player reports for toxicity. Its text filtering is off, and it does not record voice.

The owner of SW12H Studios reads reports in Unity's dashboard and can restrict an account there. When the owner is away, an AI assistant working for the owner may read and act on reports in the same way. We aim to review reports within 24 hours, sooner for serious ones. If an account is disabled, the game says so the next time that player connects, and gives our email address. We may share reports with authorities where the law requires, including apparent child sexual abuse material.

Your block list is kept on your device and with Unity Friends.

Ads

The free version shows banners, interstitials, and optional "watch an ad" rewards from Google AdMob. Ads keep running for a free-version player who is hosting visitors. Google may collect your advertising ID, app set ID, IP address (and a general location from it), ad interactions, and diagnostics. Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from apps apply. We see only totals in AdMob.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: Google's consent form asks for your choices before any ad is requested. To change them later, open Settings and choose "Ad choices" under "Ads". That row appears only where Google says it is needed. If you decline, ads may be limited, may not be personalized, or may not show at all. If no real ad is available, an optional reward uses a short ad break made by the game instead, with nothing sent to Google for it.
  • iPhone and iPad: the game asks your permission for tracking before Google's ad software starts (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, your advertising identifier is not shared. Change it any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Remove Ads: this one-time purchase stops banners and interstitial ads, and stops Google's ad software starting when the game starts. Optional reward videos stay available. Google's ad software starts only when a reward offer first appears on screen during a session, so that a video is ready if you choose one. The consent and tracking questions above can appear at that point, and Google may then collect the data described above. If the game has no age answer from you yet, Google's ad software waits for one, and a reward uses a short ad break made by the game until then. After a reinstall, you count as a free player until the store confirms your purchase, for example through "Restore purchases".
  • Age: your answer to the age question changes the ad settings the game sends to Google. See "Children and ages".

Purchases

Remove Ads and Gem bundles are sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play. The game receives a signed record of the purchase, checks it on your device, and keeps a record there so it can deliver what you bought. We do not run a server that checks purchases. We see only sales reports from the stores. Unity's store software also sends Unity the data in the "Unity In-App Purchasing" row above. See our Terms of Service for Gems and refunds.

What stays on your device

  • Your worlds and progress, your Gem balance and purchase record, your Photo Mode pictures and framed photo copies.
  • Your saved age answer, your block list, your sign-in token, your acceptance of the Terms of Service, and a technical installation ID from Unity's core software.
  • IDs and names of other players you have met: hosts remember visitors they gave permissions to and any decorations they placed; visitors' saves keep records of hosts they visited, so rewards arrive safely.
  • Friend names and phone numbers you type into the Mail Terminal.

We do not receive any of this. The game also uses your device's identifier locally to seal your purchase record; that identifier is not sent by the game for this purpose. Your device's backups: Android backup and iCloud device backup can include your saves and the game's settings, under Google's or Apple's terms. Framed photo copies and the remembered age answer are left out.

Mail Terminal, codes and links

Parcels and friend links go in a text message you send from your own messaging app. Your own number goes in a link only if you enter it. Redeem and parcel codes are checked on your device. Community buttons open Discord or YouTube in your browser, under their policies.

Children and ages

Garden Quest RPG is rated ESRB T and PEGI 18 on Google Play and 13+ on the App Store (some countries differ). It is not made for children.

The first time you start the game, including the first time after updating to this version, it asks your age before any ad: 12 and under, or 13 or older. Your answer stays on your device and is not sent to us or Unity. Google receives only the ad settings described below.

  • 12 and under: you cannot go online. Online play is switched off and no online account is created. The answer is not saved. It is held in memory only until the game closes, then the question is asked again the next time you start the game. Until then, the game asks Google for child-directed ads rated G.
  • 13 or older: online play is allowed. Voice stays off unless you turn it on yourself. The answer is remembered on the device. The game adds no age setting to its ad requests.

If you own Remove Ads, the game does not ask when it starts. The Garden Line Station asks before you go online instead.

A remembered answer applies to every world on this device, unless a world already has its own recorded answer. 12 and under applies to every world until the game closes.

Changing your answer: you can change your answer to 12 and under in Settings, under "Change my age answer", at any time. It applies right away. It closes any world or visit you are in at once, and the game asks again the next time it starts. "Delete all on-device game data" also clears your answer. Deleting your online data does not.

Google's rules for these settings limit personalized ads. Ad settings apply to the free version and to any optional reward video.

What this does not do: it is a question, not an age check, and someone can answer untruthfully. The game does not read age signals from Apple or Google.

If you believe a child under 13 has an online account, email us with the friend ID if you have it and we will delete it. Parents can also use Delete your data.

Your choices and rights

Stay offline, leave voice off, decline tracking, use "Ad choices" where it appears, or delete your data (how). To use a legal right, email us with your friend ID. We aim to answer requests within 24 hours, and we complete emailed deletion requests within 30 days. Because online accounts have no real name or email, we can only find yours by that ID.

EEA and UK

Our legal bases: contract (running the online play you ask for); legitimate interests (keeping players safe, handling reports, preventing abuse and showing ads funding the free game); consent where required (microphone, tracking and personalized ads); legal obligation where it applies.

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, or a copy of your data; object to processing based on legitimate interests; and withdraw consent at any time. You can complain to your local data protection authority. Your data may be processed in the United States and elsewhere by Unity and Google under their safeguards, such as the EU's standard contractual clauses. The game makes no automated decisions about you with legal effects.

EU Digital Services Act: our single point of contact, for users and authorities, is staycalmandgrind@gmail.com, in English. Report illegal content in the game or by email. See our Terms of Service.

California and other US states

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know, access, correct, or delete your personal information, and to opt out of its sale or sharing for targeted ads, without being treated worse for it. In the last 12 months, the categories involved are identifiers (player ID, device and advertising IDs, IP address), internet activity (ad interactions, gameplay traffic), approximate location, audio (voice), user content (chat, pictures), and purchase history (product, price, transaction ID, receipt).

We do not sell personal information for money. Google's use of identifiers for personalized ads may count as "sale" or "sharing" under some state laws. To limit this today, decline tracking on iPhone or iPad, or delete your advertising ID on Android.

Permissions

Android: network access, the microphone (asked only when you tap "Join voice"), audio and Bluetooth access for headsets during voice, and the advertising ID. iPhone and iPad: tracking (for ads, which you can decline), the microphone (asked only when you tap "Join voice"), and adding Photo Mode pictures to your photos. The game does not ask for your contacts, camera, or location.

Security

Unity's documents say that service data is encrypted in transit, with the voice exception above. Nothing online is perfectly secure. Your on-device data is as safe as your device.
