Contact us

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email staycalmandgrind@gmail.com and we will get back to you as soon as we can. Please tell us your device model (for example iPhone 15 or Moto G Play) and what happened — a screenshot helps a lot.

Remove Ads

The one-time Remove Ads purchase removes every ad for good. If you reinstall the game or move to a new phone signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account, open Settings in the game and tap Restore next to "Already bought it? Restore your purchase".

Your farm and saves

Your farm is saved on your device only. There are no accounts. Deleting the game deletes its saves, so keep it installed when you change settings or update.
